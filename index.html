About us

Multiscale Water & Flood Engineering at Kyungpook National University.

We investigate urban flood mechanisms and decision-support systems by combining HEC‑RAS 1D/2D, SWMM, FVM, and System Dynamics. Our mission is to translate scientific models into real‑world resilience for cities and watersheds.
